Intro

Hi, I'm Fishy, and welcome to the Atlantis general guide! This guide contains all (or most) of the information you need to get started. If you have any questions that weren't answered here, ask in the #atlantis-help Slack channel and we will respond as soon as we can.

Not on the Slack? Join here.

What is Atlantis?

Atlantis is a program coming to you from Hack Club! The first three weeks consist of us (the team) gathering people to participate, because once we start it's too late to join. You are also able to CAD for pearls (our currency) while you wait for the main voyage to begin.

Then starts the 8 weeks of CAD. You must do a minimum of 5 hours of CAD logged each week using Lapse (a Hack Club project timelapse recorder). Record there, publish, then tape the timelapse into a lapse in your project book. A week runs Monday 12:00 AM to Sunday 11:59 PM Eastern, and the countdown on your deck tells you how long is left in the one you're in. Miss a week and you're out: you can't ship or log new time until you buy your way back in.

Hours and pearls

The bar on your deck is this week's 5 hours. It resets every Monday, and it's the one that decides whether you stay in.

Behind it, a printer costs 40 hours: 5 a week for 8 weeks. A week you survive adds 5 towards it and never more, however much you actually did, so the only way to get there is to survive every week. The charts show how many you've banked.

Pearls work the other way round. The 5 hours you owe each week pay 1 pearl an hour, because they're already buying you printer hours. Every hour past the 5 pays 7 pearls an hour. Anything you logged before the 8 weeks began, during the prep weeks, pays the old flat rate of 8 pearls an hour and doesn't count towards the 40. Pearls arrive when a project is finalized, not when you log the time.

Streak savers

There are two in the shop, and both are applied the moment you buy them; there's nothing to hold onto for later. Each one covers one hour of a week's requirement.

  • This week's saver tops up the week you're in. Buy it before Sunday midnight if you can see you won't make the 5.
  • The missed-week saver is how you get back in after you've been dropped. It always goes on the earliest week you're short on, so if you missed a week by 3 hours you need 3 of them. You can't buy this one if you haven't missed anything.

A week rescued with savers still counts as survived, and still adds its 5 hours to the printer bar.

At the end, if you survived, you get a free 3D printer! Pick a chart from the printer maps any time during the program: nothing is spent until the 8 weeks are over, and you can change your mind as often as you like. When it's over, you claim one printer in a single go, and its pearl cost is the whole path: the entry cost of that chart plus every upgrade step leading to the one you picked. If you only did 5 hours each week and banked no extra pearls, you can choose between a bunch of "base" printers, or a grant to buy your own. The more hours approved, the better the prizes.

What is CAD?

CAD, or computer aided design, is a broad term that in this case refers to a digital 3D design program.

For the purpose of Atlantis, this does not include 3D sculpting software used for art such as Blender, or PCB design software.

When we refer to "CAD" in Atlantis it almost always means 3D design programs using a (primarily) sketch-extrude system.

Various programs are used for designing assemblies of machinery, robotics, 3D printing, and other mechanical and real world applications. The most common form of CAD uses a sketch-extrude system for creating models.
