FAQ

What is Atlantis?

Atlantis is a Hack Club CAD YSWS, where you ship 5 hours of CAD a week for 8 weeks and we ship you a free 3D printer!

Who can participate?

Anyone 13–18 (inclusive!) from anywhere in the world.

Who are we?

We are Hack Club, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit whose mission is to encourage teens to build and ship real projects.

What counts as CAD work for Atlantis?

Any 3D modeling work that uses printed parts. Printed parts must be the primary focus, but other non-electronic hardware is also allowed.

What CAD software can I use?

You are allowed to use Fusion 360, Onshape, Solidworks, or FreeCAD. See the CAD Software guide for a breakdown of the differences.

Do I need to work on one project for all 8 weeks?

Nope, you can work on as many or as few projects as you need.

How do I submit and verify my CAD hours?

Create a project on the site, then record timelapses of yourself doing CAD on Lapse, our very own timelapse recorder. Publish one there and it shows up in the picker on your project, ready to tape into a lapse. (Footage you recorded on Lookout, which Lapse replaced, is still there under the “Lookout” tab in the same picker.)

When exactly does a week start and end?

Monday 12:00 AM to Sunday 11:59 PM, Eastern time, wherever you are. Your deck shows a countdown to the end of the week you're in. An hour counts towards the week you recorded it in, not the week you taped it into your book, so footage you hang onto doesn't move into a later week.

What happens if I miss my 5-hour goal for a week?

You're out of the program when the week closes: you can't ship or log new time until you're back in. To get back in, buy missed-week streak savers from the shop; each one covers an hour, so a week you missed by 3 hours takes 3 of them, and they always go on the earliest week you're short on.

Can I buy a streak saver before I miss a week?

Yes, that's what the other one is for. The "this week" saver tops up the week you're in, so if it's Saturday and you're an hour short you can buy one rather than lose the week. Both savers apply the instant you buy them; there's nothing to stockpile.

Can I work more than 5 hours in a week?

Yes! The extra hours don't fill the printer bar any faster (that moves 5 hours a week whatever you do), but they're worth far more in pearls, at 7 pearls an hour against 2 for the hours you owe. Pearls are how you afford a better printer at the end.

How many pearls is an hour worth?

  • 8 pearls for anything logged before the 8 weeks started.
  • 2 pearls for each of the 5 hours you owe in a week.
  • 7 pearls for every hour on top of those 5.

Pearls are paid when a project is finalized, so they arrive after review rather than the moment you log the time.

What 3D printer do I get for completing Atlantis?

For the base printer, you will receive your choice of the following:

  • Bambu Lab A1 Mini
  • Creality Ender 3 V3 SE
  • Elegoo Neptune 4
  • A $250 grant to purchase any other printer

You can get better printers by doing more CAD!

Do I have to pay anything to participate or receive the printer?

Nope, it is 100% free.

Can I use CAD work I started before Atlantis?

No, all work must be done during the duration of the program.

Can I collaborate with other people on my CAD projects?

Nope, it must be independent.

What happens after I complete all 8 weeks?

Shipping closes, and claiming opens. Go to the printer charts, pick the printer you want from your chart, and claim it: that's the one moment pearls are spent on a printer, and it costs the entry price of that chart plus every upgrade leading to the one you chose. It then goes to fulfillment like any other order, and we'll be in touch on Slack.

Do I have to pick a printer chart early?

No. Picking a chart during the program is just a plan, and it costs nothing; you can switch as often as you like right up until you claim.

How do I get pearls?

You get pearls by shipping your project, based on the amount of time spent. Higher quality projects get a higher pearl multiplier.

Who owns my design?

It is yours and yours only. Hack Club doesn't have any stake in it. However, it must be open source (the strictest license we can accept is CC-BY-SA) for us to accept it.
