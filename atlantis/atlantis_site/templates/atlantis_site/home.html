FAQ

Who can participate?

Any teenager, from anywhere in the world!

How does this work?

You ship 5 hours of CAD a week for 8 weeks. We track time using timelapses!

Which 3D printer do I get?

Depends on how much CAD you do! 5 hours a week will get you a base printer - Bambu A1 Mini, Creality Ender 3 V3 SE, Elegoo Neptune 4, or a $250 grant to buy one yourself! More work = better printer!

Do I need to know CAD already?

Nope! We're open to people of all skill levels, from beginners to experts!

Is this legit?

Yes! We're Hack Club, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that encourages teens around the world to make real projects and rewards them for it!

I have more questions!

Ask in #atlantis-help on the Hack Club Slack!
